Our Story

Quality Wood,
Honest Craft

Sandip Saw Mill is a trusted timber supplier based in Bharatpur, Chitwan, Nepal. We have been delivering premium quality lumber and wood products to construction projects, furniture makers, and households for years.

Our mill uses modern sawing equipment combined with traditional craftsmanship to produce precisely cut, well-seasoned timber that meets the highest standards in Nepal's growing construction industry.
